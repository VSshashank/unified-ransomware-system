## 0. Scope of this check — read this first

I could **not load parimalamrutha.com directly.** The machine this assessment was
prepared on sits behind a restricted network policy that blocks general outbound
web traffic (it blocks `example.com` too, so this is not something specific to
the client's domain or a sign of a problem with their hosting).

So everything below is built from two things:

1. **Publicly indexed data** about the site and the business — search-engine
   indexed page titles and snippets, the Justdial business listing, and social
   profiles. These are cited in §10.
2. **A standard audit framework** for a gallery-plus-classes art business,
   benchmarked against the client's actual direct competitors.

**What this means practically:** the *strategy, module list and budget bands in
this document are sound and can be sent as-is* — they are driven by the business
model, which is well established. What still needs a 15-minute pass on the live
site before the number is final is the **current platform and how much is
salvageable**. §9 lists exactly what to check.

Anything below marked **[verified]** came from a public source. Anything marked
**[to confirm]** is an assumption that changes the quote if it turns out wrong.

## 2. What's wrong today

**The headline problem: the homepage is a placeholder.** Search engines have the
site indexed under the title *"Parimalamrutha - A Tanjore Painting Gallery"*, but
the content they captured on the homepage is just the phrase **"SOMETHING IS
HAPPENING"** — a coming-soon / under-construction screen. A `/gallery/` page
exists and is indexed. Almost nothing else is. **[verified]**

For a 17-year-old business, that is expensive. Concretely:

1. **A 17-year reputation is invisible online.** Every trust signal the business
   has earned — the legacy, the student base, the commissioned work — is absent
   from the one asset a prospect checks before spending ₹50,000 on a painting.
2. **Search traffic is being handed to competitors.** "Tanjore painting classes
   Bangalore" is high-intent commercial search. With a placeholder homepage,
   Parimalamrutha cannot rank for it, while Tanjore Collective, Kavi Art Studio,
   Chaya, Sankalp, Himanshu Art Institute and others all run full sites with
   course catalogues and online booking. **[verified]**
3. **One review after seventeen years.** A 5.0 rating from one review reads as
   "no track record", not "perfect". This is the cheapest fix on the list and one
   of the highest impact.
4. **No way to transact.** No booking, no advance payment, no structured
   enquiry — so every lead has to be caught manually over phone/WhatsApp, and
   anything that arrives outside class hours is lost. **[to confirm]**
5. **The whole online presence rests on Instagram.** Instagram is a discovery
   channel, not an asset you own. It has no search visibility, no booking, and
   no structured catalogue.

## 3. Where the money actually is

This is the part that should shape the quote, and it is where most agencies get
an art-business brief wrong.

> **Do not lead with a big e-commerce store.** Tanjore paintings are
> consultative, commissioned purchases. Almost nobody adds a ₹80,000 gold-foil
> Balaji panel to a cart and checks out. Building a large catalogue store first
> spends the budget in the wrong place.

The website's real jobs, in priority order:

1. **Sell class seats online.** Recurring, predictable, no shipping risk, and it
   scales nationally and to the NRI market through recorded courses. This is the
   single highest-ROI module.
2. **Generate qualified commission enquiries.** Not a generic "Contact Us" box —
   a structured brief (deity, size, budget band, timeline) that arrives ready to
   quote and lands in WhatsApp.
3. **Prove credibility.** Portfolio, process, the artist's story, reviews. This
   is what converts an enquiry into a ₹50,000+ order.
4. **Win local search.** Bangalore intent keywords, Google Business Profile,
   consistent listings.
5. **Then** sell the genuinely cart-friendly things: material kits, small
   ready-to-ship works, gifting and wedding return-gift ranges.

A school that already teaches also has a quietly excellent recurring product:
**selling raw materials and kits** (chetti board, 22K gold foil, jewel stones,
colours) to its own students and alumni. Low-value, high-repeat, easy to ship —
exactly what a cart is good at.

## 4. What we can add

### A. Foundation — non-negotiable

- Replace the placeholder with a real multi-page site
- Mobile-first build (the large majority of this audience arrives on a phone)
- Page structure: Home / About the artist / Gallery / Classes / Commission a
  painting / Testimonials / Blog / Contact
- Floating **WhatsApp click-to-chat** — in India this outperforms contact forms
- Fast hosting, SSL, automated backups, uptime monitoring
- Google Business Profile claim + optimisation, Maps embed, consistent
  name/address/phone across listings
- On-page SEO + schema markup (LocalBusiness, Course, Product, FAQ)
- Google Analytics 4, Search Console, Meta Pixel
- Privacy policy, terms, refund/cancellation policy — **mandatory** for Indian
  payment gateway approval, not optional paperwork

### B. Gallery that actually sells

- Categorised browsing — by deity (Krishna, Lakshmi, Ganesha, Balaji,
  Saraswati…), by size, by price band
- **High-resolution zoom.** Gold foil and stone work is the product; a small
  flat JPEG destroys the value proposition
- Per-artwork **"Enquire about this piece"** → WhatsApp/form with the artwork
  reference pre-filled
- Available / Sold / Made-to-order badges
- Work-in-progress and before-after sequences (these perform extremely well)

### C. The Academy — the revenue engine

- Course catalogue: Beginner, Advanced, Weekend, Kids, Online-live, Self-paced
- **Batch calendar with seats remaining** — scarcity drives enrolment
- **Online booking with advance payment** (Razorpay / UPI / cards)
- Material kit as a paid add-on at checkout
- Dedicated landing pages per course (strong search intent)
- Student work gallery + testimonials
- **Optional LMS** for recorded courses with drip-fed video and student logins —
  this is what unlocks selling outside Bangalore and to NRIs

### D. Commissions

- **"Commission your Tanjore painting" configurator**: deity → size → frame →
  budget band → timeline → upload a reference or a photo of the wall
- Published price bands and a clear process timeline (design → sketch → gold
  work → framing → delivery)
- Advance / milestone payment capability
- An explicit **packing and shipping policy** page — buyers of large fragile
  panels worry about this, and saying nothing loses the sale

### E. E-commerce — phase 2, and selective

- Ready-to-ship small paintings, gifting range, wedding return gifts
- **Raw materials and kits** (the recurring seller described in §3)
- Razorpay/UPI/cards, GST-compliant invoicing, COD rules
- Shipping integration (Shiprocket / Delhivery) with an insured art-packaging tier
- **International shipping** — the NRI market for devotional Tanjore art is real
  and underserved

### F. Trust and content

- The artist's story, the 17-year legacy, awards, press
- Video: studio tour, the gold-foil process, student journeys
- **An active Google review drive** — moving from 1 review to 30+ is close to free
- Photo testimonials from students and commission clients
- Blog aimed at real search demand: *How to identify genuine 22K gold foil
  Tanjore*, *Which deity faces which direction (Vastu)*, *Caring for a Tanjore
  painting*, *Tanjore vs Mysore painting*
- FAQ page

### G. Growth

- Local SEO for Bangalore commercial-intent keywords
- Google Business Profile posts
- Instagram/Threads feed integration, Reels on-site
- WhatsApp Business catalogue
- Email/WhatsApp list for batch announcements — the cheapest repeat-sales channel
- Google Ads on "Tanjore painting classes Bangalore" (media spend, separate)
- Clean up and complete the Justdial / Sulekha / UrbanPro listings

### H. Technical

- Image optimisation and page speed — critical, because a gallery site is heavy
- Accessibility basics
- Security hardening, WAF, scheduled backups
- Optional Kannada / Tamil / Hindi toggle (lower priority — the NRI and premium
  segments both search in English)

## 5. Packages and budget

All figures in INR, excluding GST. Bands reflect current Indian market rates for
2026 (sources in §10) and assume a professional agency build, not a freelancer
template job.

### Package 1 — Relaunch Essentials · **₹65,000 – ₹95,000**

Kill the placeholder and turn the site into a working lead-generator.

8–10 pages · mobile responsive · categorised gallery · class information +
enquiry forms · WhatsApp integration · Google Business Profile setup · local SEO
basics · analytics · legal/policy pages · 1 year hosting + SSL

*3–4 weeks. Good if budget is tight and the goal is simply to stop losing leads.*

### Package 2 — Academy + Gallery · **₹1,45,000 – ₹1,95,000** ← recommended

Everything in Package 1, plus the parts that actually take money:

Online class booking + payment gateway · batch calendar with seat counts ·
commission configurator · per-artwork enquiry system · testimonials and review
system · blog setup + 6 seed articles · full schema markup · speed optimisation ·
photo/video art direction · **3 months post-launch support**

*6–8 weeks. This is the version that pays for itself, because it captures class
revenue and qualified commission enquiries instead of just describing them.*

### Package 3 — Full Commerce + Online School · **₹2,75,000 – ₹3,75,000**

Everything in Package 2, plus:

WooCommerce store (paintings, kits, raw materials) · **LMS with drip-fed video
and student logins** · shipping integration incl. international · GST invoicing ·
coupons and gifting · abandoned-cart recovery · CRM + WhatsApp automation ·
multi-currency

*10–14 weeks. Right only if they commit to producing recorded course content —
the LMS is the whole reason this tier costs what it does.*

### Add-ons (any package)

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Professional artwork photography (50–80 pieces) | ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 |
| Studio/process brand film (2–3 min) + 8–10 reels | ₹40,000 – ₹75,000 |
| Logo / brand identity refresh | ₹20,000 – ₹40,000 |
| Content writing, per page | ₹1,500 – ₹3,000 |
| Additional language | ₹15,000 – ₹25,000 |
| LMS module retrofitted later, on its own | ₹60,000 – ₹1,00,000 |
| Commission configurator on its own | ₹25,000 – ₹40,000 |

**Note on photography:** for a gallery business this is not a nice-to-have.
Poorly shot gold foil looks flat and cheap, and no amount of web design fixes
it. If the budget is constrained, take it out of the build tier rather than out
of the photography.

### Platform recommendation — WordPress + WooCommerce

Shopify is the obvious alternative and the wrong call here. Shopify Payments is
not available in India, so every order carries Shopify's **extra 5% transaction
fee on the Basic plan on top of the Razorpay/PayU gateway charge**. At this
business's ticket sizes that compounds badly. WooCommerce on good Indian hosting
avoids it, and keeps the LMS and course-booking options open. **[verified]**

## 6. Running costs

| Item | Per year |
|---|---|
| Domain | ₹900 – ₹1,500 |
| Hosting — brochure/gallery site | ₹6,000 – ₹15,000 |
| Hosting — WooCommerce + LMS | ₹12,000 – ₹30,000 |
| SSL | Free (Let's Encrypt) |
| Maintenance / AMC (₹1,500–₹4,000 per month) | ₹18,000 – ₹48,000 |
| Plugin & LMS licences | ₹8,000 – ₹25,000 |

**Per transaction:** payment gateway around 2% + GST. Shipping billed per order.

**Optional, and a separate decision from the build:**

- SEO + social media retainer: **₹15,000 – ₹40,000 per month**
- Google Ads media spend: **₹10,000 – ₹30,000 per month**

Be straight with the client that the retainer is where most of the long-term
money goes. A ₹1.5L site with no ongoing work loses to a ₹70K site with a
consistent ₹15K/month behind it.

## 8. Quick wins — do these in week 1, regardless of package

These cost almost nothing and move the needle faster than the build:

1. **Take the placeholder down.** Even a clean one-page holding site with
   photos, phone number, address and a WhatsApp button beats "SOMETHING IS
   HAPPENING".
2. **Claim and fill the Google Business Profile** — photos, hours, services,
   class information.
3. **Run a review drive.** Message past students and commission clients and ask
   for a Google review. Going from 1 review to 30 is the single cheapest
   credibility gain available.
4. **Set up WhatsApp Business** with a catalogue and away-message.
5. **Put the website link in the Instagram bio** and keep it pointed at
   something worth landing on.

Roughly ₹0 – ₹15,000, and it can start before the contract is signed.

## 9. What we need to confirm before the number is final

A 15-minute pass on the live site closes every open item:

- [ ] What is the site actually built on — WordPress, Wix, a builder, or custom?
- [ ] Is the whole site a placeholder, or only the homepage? What is on
      `/gallery/` and how many other pages exist?
- [ ] Who controls the domain, the hosting and the DNS? Are the logins available?
- [ ] Does any existing content survive — photographs, written copy, a logo?
- [ ] Is there any current enquiry form, booking or payment flow?
- [ ] How does the site behave on a phone, and how slow is it?
- [ ] Is there a GST registration, and a current-account for a payment gateway?
- [ ] How many photographs of finished work exist, and at what resolution?
- [ ] Do they want to teach online, or stay studio-only? **This one decides
      between Package 2 and Package 3.**

**Recommendation:** send Package 2 as the headline proposal with Package 1 as the
budget option and Package 3 as the growth path. Lead the conversation with the
class-booking module, because that is the part with a revenue number attached to
it that the client can picture.
